FAQ

Business Reporting Frequently Asked Questions

What is business reporting?

Business reporting involves organising and presenting business information in a structured format so that performance and relevant business information can be reviewed.

Why is business reporting important?

Reporting can help businesses organise important information and make performance easier to monitor and understand.

Can you create reports from Excel data?

Yes. Excel and Power Query can be used as part of preparing and analysing business information for reporting.

Can business reporting use Power BI?

Yes. Power BI can be used to create interactive dashboards and visual reports from relevant business information.

What types of businesses can use reporting?

Businesses across many industries can use reporting to organise and review relevant operational, sales, customer, financial or other business information.
