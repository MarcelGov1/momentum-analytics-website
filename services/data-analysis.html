Data analysis helps transform available business information into findings that can help businesses understand performance, identify patterns and investigate important questions.

FAQ

Data Analysis Frequently Asked Questions

What is data analysis?

Data analysis is the process of examining business information to identify useful patterns, trends, comparisons and insights.

Why is data analysis important for a business?

Analysis can help businesses understand available information and investigate questions about performance, activity and trends.

Can you analyse Excel data?

Yes. Excel can be used to organise, prepare and analyse many types of structured business information.

Can you analyse database information?

Yes. SQL and database analysis can be used where relevant business information is stored in structured databases.

Can data analysis lead to a dashboard?

Yes. Analysis findings can be presented through appropriate reports, visualisations or business intelligence dashboards.

What information do I need to get started?

The starting point depends on your business requirement and the information currently available. Momentum Analytics can discuss your data and reporting needs with you.
