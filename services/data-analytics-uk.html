FAQ

Data Analytics Frequently Asked Questions

What is data analytics?

Data analytics involves examining and interpreting business information to identify useful trends, patterns and insights that can support business decisions.

Do you work with UK businesses remotely?

The page is intended for remote enquiries from businesses across the UK. Project scope, communication, data access and delivery arrangements can be discussed before work begins.

What type of businesses can use analytics?

Businesses across many industries can use analytics. The appropriate solution depends on the information a business collects and the questions it needs answered.

Do I need a large amount of data?

Not necessarily. The appropriate approach depends on your business requirements, the information available and what you are trying to understand.

Can you work with Excel data?

Yes. Excel and Power Query solutions can be used for data cleaning, transformation and reporting.

Can you create Power BI dashboards?

Yes. Power BI dashboards are part of Momentum Analytics' data analytics and business intelligence offering.
