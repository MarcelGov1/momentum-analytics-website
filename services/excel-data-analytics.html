FAQ

Excel Data Analytics FAQs

What is Excel data analytics?

Excel data analytics involves using spreadsheet information to investigate business performance, trends, patterns and other relevant measures.

Can you analyse my existing Excel spreadsheets?

Yes. Existing Excel spreadsheets can be reviewed according to the requirements of the project and the information contained within them.

Can Excel be used for business reporting?

Yes. Excel can be used to organise information, create reports, calculate measures and present business data visually.

Can Excel data be used in Power BI?

Excel information can form part of a Power BI workflow, depending on the structure of the data and the requirements of the project.

Can you clean my Excel data?

Relevant data-quality issues can be identified and addressed as part of an appropriate data preparation or analytics project.

What types of Excel data can be analysed?

This depends on the business. Examples can include sales, customer, financial, operational, inventory and performance information.

Do I need to be an Excel expert?

No. You can start by explaining what information you currently have and what you would like to understand from it.
