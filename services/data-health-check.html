Understand the health of your business data.

Businesses collect information every day through sales, customers, operations, finance, marketing and other business activities.

The challenge is knowing whether that information is accurate, organised, accessible and useful for business reporting and decision-making.

Momentum Analytics helps businesses review how their data is collected, managed and used, while identifying areas where data quality and reporting can potentially be improved.

FAQ

Data Health Check Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Data Health Check?

A Data Health Check is a review of how a business collects, manages and uses data, with the aim of identifying potential data quality, reporting and process improvements.

Why is data quality important?

Poor-quality or inconsistent information can make it more difficult to produce useful reports and understand business performance.

Does my business need a large amount of data?

No. The appropriate approach depends on your business requirements, the information available and what you are trying to understand.

Can Momentum Analytics help after the Data Health Check?

Yes. Depending on the requirements identified, Momentum Analytics provides services including data analysis, business intelligence, Power BI, SQL, Excel, Power Query and business reporting.

Can you work with Excel data?

Yes. Excel and Power Query are part of the Momentum Analytics data services offering.
