DATA HEALTH CHECK
UNDERSTAND YOUR DATA

Is Your Business Data Working for You?

Businesses collect data every day through sales, customers, financial records, operations, marketing and other business activities.

But having data is not the same as having useful, reliable data. A Data Health Check helps identify potential gaps in the way your business collects, stores, manages and uses information.

Momentum Analytics can help you understand where improvements can be made and where better use of your data could support business reporting and decision-making.

WHAT WE CHECK

What Does a Data Health Check Look At?

The review focuses on the way your business handles and uses data.

DQ

Data Quality

Identify potential issues such as incomplete, inconsistent, duplicated or outdated business data.

DC

Data Collection

Review how information enters your business and whether the data being collected supports useful reporting and analysis.

DM

Data Management

Look at how business information is stored, organised and maintained across your processes.

RP

Reporting

Review how your business turns data into reports and whether those reports provide useful insight.

BI

Business Intelligence

Identify opportunities to use business data more effectively for understanding performance and trends.

IM

Improvement Opportunities

Highlight areas where changes to data processes, reporting or analysis could improve the way information is used.

DATA REVIEW

Areas We Can Review

  • How your business currently collects data

FAQ

Data Health Check Frequently Asked Questions

What is a Data Health Check?

A Data Health Check is a review of how a business collects, manages and uses data, with the aim of identifying potential data quality, reporting and process improvements.

Why is data quality important?

Poor-quality or inconsistent data can make it more difficult to produce useful reports and understand business performance.

Does my business need large amounts of data?

No. Businesses of different sizes can benefit from understanding how their data is collected, managed and used.

Can Momentum Analytics help after the check?

Yes. Depending on the needs identified during the review, Momentum Analytics can provide services such as data analysis, business intelligence, Power BI, SQL, Excel and business reporting.

How do I get started?

Contact Momentum Analytics to discuss your business and request a Data Health Check.
