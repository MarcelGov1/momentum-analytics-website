Understand the Health of Your Business Data

Businesses generate information every day through sales, customers, finances, operations, employees, marketing and other business activities.

But having data is not the same as having useful data. If information is incomplete, inconsistent, difficult to access or spread across different systems, it can become difficult to understand what is actually happening in the business.

Momentum Analytics uses a Data Health Check to understand how your business currently collects, stores, manages and uses data.

During the assessment, we look at areas such as:

  • Data collection and storage
  • Data quality and consistency
  • Reporting processes
  • Business dashboards
  • Excel and spreadsheet processes
  • SQL and database information
  • Business decision-making

What Does a Data Health Check Examine?

The purpose is to identify where your current data processes are working well and where improvements could make your information more useful.

1

Data Quality

We look for issues such as missing information, inconsistent values, duplicate records and unreliable data that may affect reporting.

2

Data Collection

We examine how information enters your business and whether the current collection process gives you the information you need.

3

Reporting

We look at how your business currently produces reports and whether reporting is efficient, consistent and useful for decision-making.

4

Dashboards & Insights

We assess whether your business could benefit from clearer dashboards, visual reporting or automated access to important business information.

5

Data Systems

We discuss the tools and systems your business uses, including Excel, databases, SQL, Power BI and other reporting tools.

6

Decision-Making

We identify whether the information available to management provides the visibility needed to understand business performance.

Questions We Ask About Your Data

Every business handles information differently. These questions help us understand your current data environment.

Where Is Your Data?

Is your information stored in spreadsheets, databases, accounting systems, CRM platforms or multiple locations?

Can You Trust It?

Are your reports based on information that is accurate, complete and consistent?

How Long Does Reporting Take?

How much time does your team spend collecting, cleaning and preparing information?

What Do You Measure?

Are you tracking the business metrics that actually matter to your operations and goals?

Who Uses the Information?

Can the right people easily access the information they need when making decisions?

What Could Be Improved?

Are there repetitive processes or reporting tasks that could potentially be streamlined?

Data Health Check FAQs
