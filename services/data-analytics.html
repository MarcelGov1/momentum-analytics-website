FAQ

Data Analytics Frequently Asked Questions

What is data analytics?

Data analytics involves examining and interpreting business information to identify useful trends, patterns and insights that can support business decisions.

What type of businesses can use data analytics?

Businesses across many industries can use analytics. The appropriate solution depends on the type of information a business collects and the questions it needs answered.

Do I need a large amount of data?

Not necessarily. The appropriate approach depends on your business requirements, the information available and what you are trying to understand.

Can Momentum Analytics work with Excel data?

Yes. Momentum Analytics' existing service offering includes Excel and Power Query solutions for data cleaning, transformation and reporting.

Can you create Power BI dashboards?

Yes. Power BI dashboards are part of Momentum Analytics' data analytics and business intelligence offering.

How do I get started?

Contact Momentum Analytics to discuss your business requirements and available data. You can also request a consultation to discuss your needs.
