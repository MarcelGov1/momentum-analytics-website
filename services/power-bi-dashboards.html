POWER BI DASHBOARD SERVICES

Make your business data easier to understand.

Businesses often have valuable information stored across spreadsheets, systems and databases. Power BI can bring that information together into interactive dashboards and reports.

Momentum Analytics helps businesses turn their data into practical Power BI dashboards designed around the information they need to monitor.

Instead of working through large spreadsheets or disconnected reports, dashboards can provide a structured view of important business information, trends and key performance indicators.

The objective is to make business information clearer so that you can spend less time searching for numbers and more time understanding what those numbers mean.

FAQ

Power BI Dashboard FAQs

What is Power BI?

Power BI is a business intelligence and data visualisation platform that can be used to analyse information and create interactive reports and dashboards.

What is a Power BI dashboard?

A Power BI dashboard is a visual reporting environment that can bring selected business information and performance measures together.

Can you build a Power BI dashboard using Excel data?

Yes. Excel can be one of the sources used for a Power BI dashboard, depending on the structure and quality of the data.

Can Power BI work with databases?

Power BI can work with a range of data sources. The appropriate approach depends on the systems, data structure and requirements of the business.

Can you improve an existing Power BI dashboard?

Existing dashboards can be reviewed to identify opportunities around layout, reporting requirements, data structure and usability.

Do I need to know Power BI to use a dashboard?

You do not need to be a data analyst to discuss your reporting requirements. The dashboard can be designed around the business information you need to understand.

How do I get started?

Contact Momentum Analytics to discuss your business data, reporting requirements and the type of information you would like to monitor.
