DATA QUALITY SOUTH AFRICA

Good decisions start with reliable information.

Businesses rely on information to understand customers, monitor performance, manage operations, prepare reports and make business decisions.

When business information contains missing values, duplicates, inconsistencies or inaccuracies, it can become more difficult to produce reliable reports and meaningful analysis.

Momentum Analytics helps businesses identify potential data-quality issues and understand where improvements may be required.

UNDERSTANDING DATA QUALITY

What is Data Quality?

Data quality refers to the usefulness, accuracy, consistency and completeness of information used by a business.

AC

Accuracy

Information should represent the business information it is intended to describe as accurately as possible.

CO

Completeness

Missing information can limit analysis and make it harder to produce complete business reports.

CS

Consistency

Information should follow appropriate formats and conventions across the business wherever possible.

DU

Duplicate Data

Duplicate records can affect totals, customer information, reporting and analysis.

VA

Validation

Business information can be reviewed against appropriate rules and expected formats.

RL

Reliability

Reliable information provides a stronger foundation for business reporting and analysis.

COMMON DATA QUALITY PROBLEMS

What can affect your business data?

Data-quality problems can occur at different stages of the business data lifecycle.

01

Missing Information

Important fields may be incomplete or missing, making it difficult to analyse the full picture.

02

Duplicate Records

The same customer, transaction or business record may appear more than once.

03

Inconsistent Formats

Dates, names, categories and other fields may be recorded using different formats.

04

Incorrect Information

Incorrect values can affect calculations, reporting and business analysis.

05

Outdated Information

Information that is no longer current may reduce the usefulness of business reporting.

06

Disconnected Data

Information stored across different files or systems can make analysis and reporting more difficult.

Data Quality Frequently Asked Questions

What is data quality?

Data quality refers to characteristics such as accuracy, completeness, consistency and reliability of information used by a business.

Why is data quality important?

Business reporting and analysis depend on the information being used. Data-quality issues can make reporting and analysis more difficult.

What data can be reviewed?

Depending on your business, relevant customer, sales, financial, operational, marketing and reporting information can be considered.

Can you work with Excel data?

Yes. Excel and Power Query form part of the Momentum Analytics data services offering and can be used for data cleaning, transformation and analysis.

Can data quality affect Power BI dashboards?

The information used by a dashboard affects the analysis and visualisations produced from that information. Reviewing data quality can therefore be useful when preparing data for reporting and dashboards.
