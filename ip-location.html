Privacy Notice

When you perform a lookup, the IP address is sent to external IP geolocation services so that location and network information can be returned.

Momentum Analytics does not present this tool as an exact tracking system. The results are intended for educational, informational and cybersecurity-awareness purposes.

Do not use IP information to harass, threaten, stalk or target another person.
