Privacy Notice

This tool is intended for legitimate educational, cybersecurity awareness and network-information purposes. The IP address entered into this tool is sent to a third-party IP geolocation service to retrieve the displayed information.

Do not use this tool to harass, threaten, stalk or otherwise target another person. An IP address should not be treated as proof of someone's exact physical location or identity.
